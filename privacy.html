Privacy Policy — Thriceforged

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Thriceforged (the "game") is a paid game made by Soohwan Kim (the "developer"). This page explains what information the game handles.

Information the developer collects

None. The game has no account sign-up and the developer runs no servers. The game does not collect or transmit personal information such as your name, email, location, contacts, or advertising identifier.

Information stored on your device

Your run in progress, records, unlocks, feats, and settings (language, sound, vibration) are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

Leaderboards (Game Center · Google Play Games)

The global ranking for Bellows mode is provided through Apple's Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android). Only if you are signed in to that service, the game submits the turn you reached to that service's leaderboard. The leaderboard shows your nickname on that service and your score. Apple or Google processes this information under its own privacy policy, and the developer receives nothing beyond the nickname and score shown on the leaderboard. Everything in the game works without signing in.

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Ads and analytics

The game contains no ads, no analytics tools, and no third-party tracking.

Payments

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. The developer never receives your payment details.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
